Questions

Straight answers

How much does it cost?
Nothing during launch. There's no monthly fee while we build the network — early mechanics lock in their spot before anyone else.
Do you take a cut of my jobs?
No. You set your price, the customer pays you directly, and you keep 100% of it. During launch there are no fees of any kind. If that ever changes, early mechanics hear it from us first — no surprises.
Do I need my own shop?
No. Work mobile, out of your own shop or home garage, or both. If you've got the tools and the space, you've got a shop.
How do jobs reach me?
Customers post jobs and diagnostic requests in real time. They land in your feed with the vehicle, the issue, and the distance — you respond with your price and availability.
