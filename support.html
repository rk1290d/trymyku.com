Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post a job?

Tap the Post Job button on the home screen, fill in your vehicle details, describe the issue, select your location and urgency, and submit. Mechanics in your area will respond with bids.

How do I request a diagnosis?

When posting a job select "I need a diagnosis" and describe your symptoms. You can request ASAP or scheduled service. Mechanics will respond with their availability and diagnostic fee.

How are mechanics verified?

All mechanics are required to upload a government-issued ID which is reviewed and approved by the Myku team before they can accept jobs.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your Profile, tap Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data.
