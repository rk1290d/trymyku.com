What mechanics ask us

What does it cost to be on Myku?
Nothing during launch. No subscription, no sign-up fee while we build the network in your area. Founding mechanics keep that head start and get first pick of jobs as demand ramps up.
Are there really customers, or is this just another lead list?
That's exactly why we recruit mechanics first. We're building real supply in DuPage County before we drive customer demand, so the jobs that show up are real people in your area — not recycled leads sold to ten shops at once.
How and when do I get paid?
You set your price on every quote. The customer picks you, you do the work, and you get paid for the job — no bidding your rate into the ground and no monthly fee eating your margin.
Do I need my own shop?
No. Work fully mobile and go to the customer, run everything out of your own shop, or do both. You set your service area and the kinds of jobs you take.
Do I have to accept every job?
Never. You see the vehicle, the problem, and the location before you respond, and you only quote the jobs you actually want. Your schedule stays yours.
