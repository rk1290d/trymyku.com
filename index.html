It's a random morning. The car just won't start.

Everything looks fine. No warning lights, no obvious clue. Your only real move is texting around, hoping someone knows a mechanic you can actually trust.

Most people don't have that friend. So they get stranded, towed, or quietly overcharged by whoever they could find first.

Myku is the friend-of-a-friend, for everyone. A vetted mechanic in your pocket, whether they come to you or you bring the car to them.

How it works

Fixed in three steps

Know exactly what's wrong, or have no idea at all. Either way, Myku connects you with the right mechanic.

1

Post or describe it

Know the repair? Post the job. Not sure what's wrong? Describe the symptoms and request a diagnosis.

2

Mechanics respond

Nearby mechanics send quotes and availability. Compare prices and reviews side by side.

3

Choose who fixes it

Pick the mechanic you trust. They come to you, or you drop off at their shop. Your call.

Why Myku
