Launching on iOS · Currently building our mechanic network in DuPage County, IL

Finding a mechanic used to mean knowing someone. A friend of a friend. A lucky Google search. A gamble on whoever showed up. We built Myku Auto because that's not good enough.

Myku Auto is a marketplace that connects vehicle owners with independent mechanics in their area. Customers post jobs or describe symptoms and request a diagnosis. Mechanics respond with their price and availability. Everyone wins.
